Entry Models

4 defined setups.
Zero ambiguity.

B S Confirmation Entry

The Bread & Butter

Price pulls back into the SMA 9/21 zone, all 5 SMAs are stacked in the direction of your bias, then closes back through the zone. Enter on candle close. This is the highest volume setup and the backbone of the strategy.

All 5 SMAs bullishly/bearishly stacked in order
Price pulls into SMA 9/21 confluence zone
Candle closes back above/below the zone → entry
SL placed below the session box low / last swing low
Standard Signal · Target 1:3+ RR
E Early Entry

Catching the Breakout

A strong breakout candle through the SMA zone while SMA 9 is curling sharply. Enter on the breakout candle close — but at half position size, because confirmation hasn't printed yet. Only valid inside the Session Open Window.

Strong momentum candle closes through the SMA zone
SMA 9 visibly curling in direction of breakout
Only inside the Session Open Window (first 4 hours)
½ Position Size · Higher RR Potential
Sweep

The Liquidity Hunt

Price wicks beyond the Asia session high or low — hunting the stop orders clustered there — then closes back inside the Asia range. This is a textbook institutional sweep. Enter on the close of the candle that sweeps and reverses.

Price wicks beyond Asia High or Asia Low
Candle closes back inside the Asia range → entry
SL: beyond the absolute wick extreme (fixed max applies)
70–75% Win Rate · Highest Documented
BUY SELL

Maximum Confluence Signal

The rarest and highest-confidence signal. A Sweep entry is accompanied by RSI divergence within 3 candles. When the market hunts stops AND shows a momentum divergence simultaneously — this is the cleanest read of institutional intent in the strategy.

Liquidity Sweep candle closes back inside Asia range
RSI divergence present within 3 candles of the sweep
White BUY or SELL label confirms the combo on indicator
★ Rarest Signal · Target 1:4+ RR
