Setup
How to Access the Indicator

The Silver Apex indicator is a custom TradingView tool built specifically around this strategy. Search for Silver Apex Strategy in the TradingView indicator library and add it to your chart.

RECOMMENDED SETUP
Open TradingView → search XAG/USD (Silver) or XAU/USD (Gold)
Set your chart to 5m or 15m timeframe — these give the cleanest signals
Click Indicators → search Silver Apex Strategy → Add to chart
The indicator automatically overlays on your price chart with sessions, SMAs and signals
Step 1
Reading the Chart — What Everything Means
SESSION BOXES — The coloured boxes show which trading session is active, drawn to the exact price range of that session.
🔴 Red box = Tokyo session (00:00–08:00 GMT) — Mark the high and low. Do not trade here.
🔵 Blue box = London session (07:00–16:00 GMT) — Active session. Look for setups.
🟡 Amber box = London Kill Zone (07:00–09:00 GMT) — Highest probability window.
🟣 Purple box = New York session + NY Kill Zone (12:00–14:00 GMT) — Second best window.
SMA STACK — Five moving averages plotted on your chart. When they are stacked in order, the trend is confirmed.
Green = SMA 9 (fastest — shows immediate momentum)
Black = SMA 21 (short term trend)
Red = SMA 50 (intermediate trend — must point in your direction)
Blue = SMA 200 (major trend — price above = bullish only)
Purple = SMA 800 (macro regime — rarely crossed)
ASIA RANGE LINES — Two red dashed lines showing the Tokyo session high and low. These are your key reference levels for the day.
Step 2
Signal Guide — What Each Label Means
B
Buy Signal — Confirmation Entry
What it means: Price pulled back into the SMA 9/21 zone and closed bullishly above it. All 5 SMAs are stacked in bullish order. This is the Confirmation Entry model.
Entry model: Confirmation Entry (65–70% win rate, 1:3–4 RR)
How to enter: Buy on the close of the candle that produced the B signal. Do not enter mid-candle.
Stop loss: Place SL below the lowest point of the current session box — NOT just below the entry candle. Session boxes act as your support zone.
Fixed SL guide: Silver max 0.50 points · Gold max 12 points · EUR/USD max 12 pips
Take profit: Minimum 1:3 RR from your entry. Identify the next key resistance level above and use that as your target.
Best context: B signal inside a Kill Zone (amber or purple box) = highest probability. B signal outside Kill Zone = still valid but use discretion.
S
Sell Signal — Confirmation Entry
What it means: Price pulled back into the SMA 9/21 zone and closed bearishly below it. All 5 SMAs are stacked in bearish order. This is the bearish Confirmation Entry.
Entry model: Confirmation Entry (65–70% win rate, 1:3–4 RR)
How to enter: Sell on the close of the candle that produced the S signal.
Stop loss: Place SL above the highest point of the current session box. Fixed max: Silver 0.50 · Gold 12pts · EUR/USD 12 pips.
Take profit: Minimum 1:3 RR. Identify next key support below as your target.
Best context: S signal with Bear Div above = very strong. S signal inside Kill Zone = highest probability.
E
Early Entry — Breakout Signal
What it means: Price has broken strongly through the SMA zone without a pullback. SMA 9 is curling with conviction. This is the Early Entry model — lower win rate but catches strong momentum moves.
Entry model: Early Entry (50–55% win rate, 1:4–6 RR potential)
How to enter: Enter on the close of the E candle. Use half your normal position size — this entry is riskier.
Stop loss: Just below/above the entry candle low/high. Keep it tight — the high RR compensates for the lower win rate.
Only fires in first half of London (07:00–11:00 GMT) or NY (12:00–16:00 GMT) — designed to catch session opening momentum moves.
B / S
Gold Signal — SMA 50/200 Bounce
What it means: Price has bounced off the SMA 50 or SMA 200 with a strong candle and full SMA alignment. This fires outside Kill Zones and catches trend continuation moves from major SMA levels.
Entry model: Confirmation Entry variation — bounce from major SMA level
How to enter: Enter on the close of the gold signal candle.
Stop loss: Below/above the SMA level that was touched. If it bounced off SMA 50 — SL goes just below SMA 50.
Best context: Gold B/S signal that aligns with a Kill Zone on a higher timeframe = strongest version of this setup.
Sweep
Liquidity Sweep Signal
What it means: Price wicked beyond the Asia session high or low then closed back inside. Institutions hunted stops then reversed. This is the Liquidity Sweep entry model — highest win rate at 70–75%.
Entry model: Liquidity Sweep (70–75% win rate, 1:3–5 RR)
How to enter: Enter on the close of the Sweep candle — the wick IS your signal. Never enter mid-wick.
Stop loss: Beyond the absolute extreme of the sweep wick — this is the true invalidation point. Fixed max applies.
Sweep signal alone is strong. Sweep + Bull/Bear Div within 3 bars = BUY/SELL combo signal — highest confidence trade on the entire indicator.
BUY / SELL
Combo Signal — Highest Confidence
What it means: A Liquidity Sweep AND RSI Divergence both fired within 3 bars of each other. This is the rarest and highest confidence signal on the indicator.
Entry model: Liquidity Sweep + RSI Divergence confluence — treat this as a priority entry every time it appears.
How to enter: Enter on the close of the candle that produced the white BUY/SELL label.
Stop loss: Beyond the sweep wick extreme. This signal justifies a slightly wider stop because the conviction is high.
Take profit: Target 1:4 or higher. The combination of sweep + divergence typically produces larger moves. Consider partial close at 1:3 then let the rest run.
Do NOT close a running B trade just because a white SELL appears. Let your existing trade hit TP first — the combo is a new trade opportunity, not an exit signal for your current position.
Bull Div
Bear Div
RSI Divergence Labels
What it means: RSI momentum is diverging from price. Bull Div = price made a lower low but RSI made a higher low — buyers are stepping in. Bear Div = price made a higher high but RSI made a lower high — sellers are stepping in.
IMPORTANT: Div labels alone are NOT entry signals. They are warnings that momentum is shifting. Do not enter on a Div label without SMA confirmation.
How to use: See a Bull Div? Watch for the next B signal to confirm. See a Bear Div? Watch for the next S signal to confirm.
Div label + B or S signal = strong confluence. Div label + Sweep = BUY/SELL combo. Div label alone = wait.
Step 3
HTF Confirmation — Always Check the Higher Timeframe First

The indicator runs on your entry timeframe (5m or 15m) but every trade must be confirmed by the higher timeframe. A B signal on 5m that goes against the Daily trend is a low probability trade.

STEP 1 — Open the Daily chart. Is price above or below SMA 200? Above = bullish bias only. Below = bearish bias only.
STEP 2 — Drop to 4H chart. Are the SMAs stacked in the same direction as the Daily? Mark your key S/R levels here.
STEP 3 — Drop to 1H chart. Confirm the session structure. Where is the Asia range? Which direction did London open?
STEP 4 — Now open your 5m or 15m chart with the indicator. Only take B signals that align with your Daily and 4H bias.
RULE: Never take a B signal on 5m if the Daily and 4H are bearish. Never take an S signal on 5m if the Daily and 4H are bullish. The HTF always wins.
BEST SETUP: Daily bullish + 4H bullish + London KZ active + B signal on 5m = all four timeframes aligned = highest probability trade of the day.
Step 4
Stop Loss & Take Profit — Fixed Rules
FIXED MAXIMUM STOP LOSS — Never exceed these distances regardless of where the natural level is. If the natural SL requires more than the maximum — skip the trade.
Silver XAG/USD — Maximum SL: 0.50 points
Gold XAU/USD — Maximum SL: 12 points
EUR/USD / GBP/USD — Maximum SL: 12 pips
KILL ZONE ENTRIES: SL goes below/above the entire session box low/high — not just the entry candle. Price frequently wicks through the candle before reversing.
SWEEP ENTRIES: SL goes beyond the absolute extreme of the sweep wick. That wick IS the invalidation point.
MINIMUM RR: Never enter a trade where you cannot identify a clear 1:3 target on the chart BEFORE entering. If the target is not visible — do not trade.
PARTIAL CLOSE STRATEGY: At 1:2 RR — close 50% of position and move SL to breakeven. Let remaining 50% run to 1:4 or beyond. This guarantees a profitable trade regardless of what happens next.
BREAKEVEN RULE: Once a trade reaches 1R profit — move SL to entry price immediately. You now have a risk-free trade.
Full Process
Step by Step — From Chart Open to Trade Execution
1. Check economic calendar — any high impact news in the next 2 hours? If yes on Silver/Gold — do not trade within 30 minutes of the event.
2. Open Daily chart — note SMA 200 direction. This is your bias for the day. Write it down.
3. Open 4H chart — confirm SMAs agree with Daily. Mark 2–3 key levels.
4. Open 5m or 15m chart with indicator — wait for session boxes to appear.
5. Note the Asia session box high and low — these are your key intraday levels.
6. Wait for London KZ (07:00–09:00 GMT) or NY KZ (12:00–14:00 GMT) to open.
7. Watch for B, S, E or white BUY/SELL signal to appear — only act if it aligns with your Daily and 4H bias.
8. Run the 8-point checklist tab before entering. All 8 must pass.
9. Enter on candle close. Set SL immediately. Set TP at minimum 1:3.
10. Move SL to breakeven at 1R profit. Partial close at 2R. Let remainder run to full target.
11. Do not stare at the trade. Check every 15–30 minutes only. Trust the process.
