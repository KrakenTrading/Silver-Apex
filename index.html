Reading the Chart
Session Boxes & SMAs
SESSION BOXES — Coloured boxes showing which trading session is active.
🔴 Red box = Tokyo (00:00–08:00 GMT) — Mark high/low. Do not trade here.
🔵 Blue box = London (07:00–15:00 GMT) — Active session. Look for setups.
🟡 Amber box = London Kill Zone (07:00–09:00 GMT) — Highest probability window.
🟣 Purple box = NY Kill Zone (12:00–14:00 GMT) — Second best window.
SMA STACK — Five moving averages. All must be in order to confirm trend.
Green=SMA 9   Black=SMA 21   Red=SMA 50   Blue=SMA 200   Purple=SMA 800
ASIA RANGE LINES — Red dashed lines marking Tokyo session high and low.
Signal Guide
What Each Label Means
B Buy Signal — Confirmation Entry
Price pulled back into SMA 9/21 zone, all 5 SMAs bullishly stacked, closed above zone. Enter on candle close.
SL: Below Kill Zone box low. TP: Minimum 1:3 RR.
S Sell Signal — Confirmation Entry
Price pulled back into SMA 9/21 zone, all 5 SMAs bearishly stacked, closed below zone. Enter on candle close.
SL: Above Kill Zone box high. TP: Minimum 1:3 RR.
E Early Entry — Breakout Signal
Strong breakout candle through SMA zone, SMA 9 curling sharply. Use HALF position size.
Only fires in first half of London (07:00–11:00 GMT) or NY (12:00–16:00 GMT).
Sweep Liquidity Sweep — 70–75% Win Rate
Price wicked beyond Asia High/Low then closed back inside. Enter on candle close. Best documented win rate.
SL: Beyond absolute sweep wick extreme. Fixed max applies.
BUY / SELL Combo — Highest Confidence Signal
Sweep + RSI Divergence within 3 bars. Rarest and highest confidence signal. Target 1:4+ RR.
Bull Div Bear Div RSI Divergence Labels
WARNING LABELS ONLY — NOT standalone entry signals. Divergence + B/S signal = strong confluence. Divergence alone = wait.
HTF Confirmation
Always Check First — The HTF Always Wins
STEP 1 — Daily chart: Price above SMA 200 = bullish bias only. Below = bearish bias only.
STEP 2 — 4H chart: SMAs stacked same direction as Daily? Mark S/R levels here.
STEP 3 — 1H chart: Confirm session structure. Where is the Asia range?
STEP 4 — 5m/15m chart with indicator: Only take signals that align with Daily and 4H bias.
RULE: Never take a B signal on 5m if Daily and 4H are bearish. The HTF always wins.
BEST SETUP: Daily bullish + 4H bullish + London KZ + B signal on 5m = highest probability.
Full Execution
Step-by-Step Process
1. Check economic calendar — high impact news in next 2 hours? Avoid 30 min either side.
2. Open Daily chart — note SMA 200 direction. This is your bias for the day.
3. Open 4H chart — confirm SMAs agree with Daily. Mark 2–3 key levels.
4. Open 5m or 15m with indicator. Note Asia session box high and low.
5. Wait for London KZ (07:00–09:00 GMT) or NY KZ (12:00–14:00 GMT) to open.
6. Watch for B, S, E or white BUY/SELL signal aligned with your bias.
7. Run the 8-point checklist. All 8 must pass.
8. Enter on candle close. Set SL immediately. Set TP at minimum 1:3.
9. Move SL to breakeven at 1R profit. Partial close at 2R. Let rest run.
10. Check every 15–30 minutes only. Trust the process.
How to Backtest
Building Your Edge Through Backtesting
Backtesting is how you build confidence in the strategy BEFORE risking real money.
Open TradingView → Go back 3–6 months on Silver 5m or 15m chart.
Add the Silver Apex Strategy indicator to the chart.
Replay price bar by bar using the TradingView bar replay feature.
Every time a B, S, E or Sweep signal appears — run the 8-point checklist.
If all 8 pass — record the trade. Entry, SL, TP, outcome, RR achieved.
Target 100 backtested trades minimum before going live.
After 100 trades: Calculate win rate, average RR, best sessions, best entry models. This is your personal edge profile.
